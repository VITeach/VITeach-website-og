Shraddha's Tale


Shraddha Pandey’s journey in VITeach is a remarkable tale of passion, dedication, and leadership. From joining as a general member to leading the club as its president, her experience exemplifies how commitment to a cause can bring about meaningful change. VITeach, a student-led initiative at VIT, focuses on providing educational opportunities to underprivileged children, and Shraddha’s journey within the club highlights both personal growth and community impact.

Her involvement with VITeach began nearly two and a half years ago when she was inspired by the club’s mission of teaching children from government schools. She had always been passionate about social service, and the opportunity to contribute to bridging educational gaps deeply resonated with her. As a general member, she participated in various departments such as school, content, and outreach, gaining hands-on experience in creating learning resources and conducting sessions. She contributed to developing worksheets and PowerPoint presentations on subjects like spoken English and mathematics, ensuring that children had access to well-structured educational materials. Additionally, she was involved in conducting Python programming sessions for students , aiming to equip them with valuable technical skills that could open doors for their future.

As Shraddha’s dedication and contributions became more significant, she advanced into the junior core committee, taking on more strategic responsibilities. Her role as the school JCC required her to coordinate educational programs, manage volunteers, and oversee the smooth execution of initiatives. This experience helped her develop leadership and organizational skills, preparing her for even bigger responsibilities. Eventually, her commitment and ability to lead effectively led her to become the president of VITeach. In this capacity, she managed a team of 10 department heads and over 500 members, overseeing all club operations, planning events, handling finances, and liaising with faculty members to ensure the club’s smooth functioning.

One of the initiatives closest to her heart was an event where VITeach hosted the children of VIT’s housekeeping staff for a full day of learning and fun. The event was meticulously planned to provide an engaging educational experience, filled with interactive sessions designed to inspire and motivate these children. From coordinating volunteers to structuring the event’s agenda, Shraddha played a crucial role in ensuring its success. The joy and excitement on the children’s faces, along with the gratitude expressed by their parents, made all the effort worthwhile and reaffirmed her belief in the power of education to transform lives.


However, leading such a large and diverse club was not without its challenges. Managing a team of over 500 members meant dealing with different perspectives, ideas, and sometimes conflicting opinions. Shraddha had to navigate these challenges with diplomacy and patience. She ensured open communication and transparency within the team, encouraging discussions where every member’s voice was heard. To avoid conflicts and ensure fair decision-making, she often relied on democratic processes such as polls and consensus-building discussions. By fostering an environment of respect and collaboration, she was able to keep the team aligned with the club’s mission while also ensuring smooth operations.

Beyond managing the internal workings of the club, Shraddha also focused on expanding VITeach’s outreach and impact. Under her leadership, the club explored new ways to enhance its teaching methodologies and increase engagement. They leveraged digital platforms to reach a wider audience and introduced new educational initiatives to cater to the evolving needs of students. By continuously innovating and adapting, VITeach was able to maximize its impact and provide better learning opportunities for underprivileged children.

Shraddha’s experience as president also reinforced the importance of mentorship and guidance. She made it a priority to engage with younger members, providing them with the necessary support and encouragement to take on leadership roles in the future. She believed in nurturing a culture where everyone felt valued and motivated to contribute. By empowering juniors and instilling confidence in them, she ensured that VITeach would continue to thrive even after her tenure.

Reflecting on her journey, Shraddha finds immense fulfillment in the work she has done with VITeach. She deeply believes in the club’s mission of using one’s privileges to create opportunities for others, particularly in education. She encourages juniors to join the club, emphasizing that no prior experience is needed—just a willingness to contribute and a passion for helping others. She firmly believes that every effort counts, and together, students can create meaningful change in the lives of underprivileged children.

Her journey in VITeach has not only allowed her to make a difference in the lives of others but has also shaped her into a more confident and capable leader. The challenges she faced, the decisions she made, and the impact she witnessed have all contributed to her personal growth. Through this experience, she has learned the value of teamwork, the importance of resilience, and the power of education in shaping futures.

Shraddha Pandey’s story serves as an inspiration to students who wish to make a difference through social initiatives. It highlights how dedication, leadership, and a strong sense of purpose can drive positive change in a community. Her time at VITeach will always remain a significant part of her journey, leaving behind a legacy of service, impact, and inspiration for future members to carry forward.

By: Chirag Srivastava
